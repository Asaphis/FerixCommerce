What changed and why

Account dashboard for the customer app. Same brand language (bone / ink / ember, sharp 2–3px corners, mono micro-labels) — but rebuilt around what a shopper actually opens their account for.

1 · Tracking first

  • The old page opened with a greeting and four equal stat tiles. The open order is now the hero: status, 4-step progress, ETA, carrier, contents, “Track package”.
  • Baymard's self-service benchmark finds order tracking is the single most-requested account feature, and it should stay inside the site — not be pushed to a courier page. Tracking and courier detail live on-page.

2 · Mobile = bottom tab bar

  • Header drops from three stacked rows to one 56px bar (mark + screen title + search + alerts). The old header nav is gone on small screens.
  • Navigation moves to a 5-item bottom tab bar — Home, Browse, Cart, Orders, Account — in the thumb zone, with an ember indicator on the active tab and the cart badge always visible.
  • Account sections (Orders, Saved, Addresses, Reviews, Payment, Settings) become a sticky chip strip instead of a wrapped button wall.
  • Content gets pb-28 so the tab bar never covers the last row, plus env(safe-area-inset-bottom) for iPhone home-bar devices.

3 · Less content, smaller voice

  • Killed the title + paragraph pairs. A section now has one heading, and every supporting line is a 9.5–12.5px mono micro-label.
  • Order rows abbreviate to “Merino wool overshirt +1 more · 2 Oct” — the full item list is one tap away in the expanded row.
  • Four address cards, a 3-line spend footnote and a duplicated “Saved items” section were replaced by one 3-cell details strip: ships to · payment · returns.

4 · Every account path on the dashboard

  • Desktop gets a sticky sidebar listing all seven account areas with live counts; mobile reaches the same list through the chip strip.
  • Baymard: 52% of users navigate to account features from the dashboard, and 78% of sites fail to expose them all. Returns, help and payment are now first-class entries.

5 · Responsive by construction

  • <640 single column, 2×2 stat grid, horizontal scroll rails (snap), tab bar.
  • 640–1023 two-column card grids, chip strip retained, tab bar retained.
  • ≥1024 232px sticky sidebar + content, 4-up stats, 4-up saved grid, hero splits into order / parcel columns.
