# Customers

## Purpose

Customer management interface. Merchants view and manage customers who have interacted with their business.

## Key Features

- **Customer list** — searchable, filterable list of customers
- **Customer profile** — contact info, addresses, order history
- **Order history** — all orders placed by customer
- **Spending** — lifetime value, average order value
- **Customer notes** — add internal notes
- **Customer segmentation** — tags, groups, custom segments
- **Activity timeline** — recent customer actions

## Data

- Customer contact information
- Customer order history
- Lifetime spending
- Saved addresses
- Customer notes and tags

## Notes

Merchant-only. Shows only customers who have purchased from this merchant's store.
